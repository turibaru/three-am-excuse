# The 3 AM Excuse

A single line in the voice of a hardware wallet that will not sign a transaction at 3 AM. The wallet has its own screen, its own memory of who set its PIN, and its own opinion about the face staring back at it in the small hours — and it is not shy about sharing that opinion.

> Look at my screen: that's your face at 3 AM, and even I can see you're not the person who set my PIN.

The excuse works because the wallet does the recognising itself: it looks at its owner through its own little screen and decides the 3 AM version of that person is a stranger. No battery joke, no firmware update, no cold-storage line — just a device that knows its owner by daylight and refuses to pretend otherwise at 3 AM.

Built for the [The 3 AM Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878) bounty on make.fun. Run `node main.ts` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set and the line is copied to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
